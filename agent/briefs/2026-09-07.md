## DEFECT FLAGGED — this lab's own denominator
The base rate weights **every UTC day equally regardless of how many minutes stood behind it.**
Median coverage is **1,201 scored minutes/day**, but 09-04 had **417** and 09-05 had **520** — and
09-05, a 43%-coverage day, is now the single highest reading in the book and one of the eight days
that *define* the base rate. A 54.8% read on 520 minutes carries a binomial sd near **2.2pp**, so its
excess over the 51.00% threshold is under two sd. Thin days therefore inflate **both** tails while
counting as full observations. This is Firm Brain §4 one level down: not "rows are not observations"
but *days are not equal observations*. If the council agrees, the fix is a coverage-weighted or
minimum-coverage base rate — a change to a pre-registered rule, so a **ruling**, not a tweak
(REG-PP-001). Nothing was adjusted this run.
