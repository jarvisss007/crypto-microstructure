**And that clean sweep is close to worthless, which is the finding.** `gap = happened − said`. This forecaster's `happened` sits within a point or two of 0.50 in *every* bin because its resolution is ~0.0001. When happened ≈ 0.50 and said is far from 0.50, the sign of the gap is **arithmetically forced** — positive below 0.5, negative above it. A test 14 of 14 bins pass by construction is not a test. It is the shape insider-radar found in its own book this morning, in a different lab, on the same day.
